## Product Purpose
Family Spy is the separate customer website at `family-spy.ru` for two independent products: Family VPN and Sufler. Each offer has its own factual purpose, price or useful pricing destination and direct next action. VPN continues to subscription and account flows; Sufler continues to its existing download and trial flow. Support is split by product. Legal terms, privacy notices and setup remain in the relevant product pages. The homepage footer contains only the existing support email. Family Spy does not create a bundle, joint subscription or combined-service promise, and does not carry the owner's portfolio, photograph, biography or employer contacts.

No USD payment provider or backend flow is introduced.
