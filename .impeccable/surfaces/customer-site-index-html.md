# Family Spy customer storefront

**Mode: Persuade.** Scope is the separate `family-spy.ru` homepage and its generated customer shell. A first-time service customer should understand VPN and call prompts, choose one product, inspect setup/terms, then use the established download or account flow. The customer PRODUCT.md owns product truth; its DESIGN.md owns this homepage's system. The root portfolio documents and portfolio source are incumbent references, not editing targets.

The owner pins an adaptation of the approved black/Golos/coral main-site composition. The first viewport contains the compact brand, Services/Help and RU/EN controls, a direct offer and setup-help sentence, a visible service-choice action, then two equal product covers. The reading order continues through descriptions, real prices or their fallback destinations, product actions, setup help and legal links. On mobile the products stack in VPN → Sufler order.

The memorable moment is the pairing of the existing VPN light portal with a readable, explicitly labelled Sufler prompt example. The portal is product artwork; the example is illustrative text, not a native screenshot. Keep both identities recognizable while making the next action easy to find. No person, portrait, employer biography or job-contact section belongs here.

Help means checking connection and setup, with the existing product support destinations. Do not enlarge it into refund, uptime, anonymity or SLA claims. Service pricing is read from fixed public sources; if it is unavailable, retain useful pricing links. Product pages keep their existing UI and legal/operator facts. Authenticated cabinet/admin and Sufler purchase/recovery continue on the established Family Pie origin; this storefront does not migrate accounts or native applications.
