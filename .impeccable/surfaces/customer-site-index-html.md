# Family Spy customer storefront

**Mode: Persuade.** Scope is the separate `family-spy.ru` homepage and its generated customer shell. A first-time customer should understand each independent product, inspect its price/setup/terms, then use its established download or account flow. The customer PRODUCT.md owns product truth; its DESIGN.md owns this homepage's system. The root portfolio documents and portfolio source are incumbent references, not editing targets.

The owner pins an adaptation of the approved black/Golos/coral main-site composition. The first viewport contains the neutral Family Spy brand, direct VPN/Sufler/Help navigation and RU/EN controls, then the standalone Family VPN offer. Sufler follows in its own full-width section. Each section pairs its own name, factual purpose, description, real price or fallback, primary product action, secondary account/setup action and terms/privacy with its own media. At mobile widths its copy precedes its media. There is no combined headline, service-choice CTA, side-by-side catalog or bundle claim.

The memorable moment belongs to each product: the existing VPN light portal supports VPN's offer; the readable, explicitly labelled Sufler prompt example supports Sufler's offer. The portal is product artwork; the example is illustrative text, not a native screenshot. Keep each identity and next action clear within its own section. No person, portrait, employer biography or job-contact section belongs here.

Support is split into named VPN and Sufler articles with their existing destinations. Help means checking connection/settings for VPN and audio setup for Sufler. Do not enlarge it into refund, uptime, anonymity or SLA claims. Each price is read from its fixed public source; if unavailable, retain its useful pricing destination. Product pages keep their existing UI and legal/operator facts. Authenticated cabinet/admin and Sufler purchase/recovery continue on the established Family Pie origin; this storefront does not migrate accounts or native applications.
