03What data is processed

On the device. 04Data transfer during food recognition

If you have enabled AI food recognition, the text or photo of a dish is transmitted to Google Gemini (Google LLC, servers in the USA) to identify the foods. This is a cross-border data transfer. Calories and macronutrients are calculated from a built-in database, not by the neural network. The journal, mood, weight and any other records are not transmitted. Recognition is off by default, is enabled manually, and is revoked in settings. Before sending, the photo is downscaled and geotags are removed.

Health and mental-state data (thought journal, mood, weight) is processed in plaintext on the device. The operator and third parties do not receive its readable content: it is not transmitted to the AI food-recognition service, and it enters the encrypted backup and sync only as unreadable ciphertext, to which the operator has no key.
